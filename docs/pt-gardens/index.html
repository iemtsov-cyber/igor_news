Investment opportunity

An operating hospitality asset with several revenue streams.

P.T. Gardens Hotel is located in Walgama / Biyagama, approximately 1 km from the Biyagama Free Trade Zone, around 45 minutes from Colombo and approximately one hour from Bandaranaike International Airport. The hotel has operated since 1992.

Corporate demand

Proximity to the Biyagama Free Trade Zone supports corporate accommodation, meetings and long-stay demand.

Events platform

Four function halls provide capacity for weddings, conferences, seminars, ceremonies and private events.

Hospitality mix

Accommodation, food & beverage, events and corporate services operate as complementary revenue lines.

A substantial hotel and events property positioned close to one of Sri Lanka's major industrial zones.
Asset & venues

Hotel, banquet and corporate infrastructure in one property.

The property includes 27 guest rooms, restaurant and bar, swimming pool, landscaped grounds, event stages, balconies and terraces, a large roof terrace, corporate meeting and catering facilities, parking and service infrastructure.
