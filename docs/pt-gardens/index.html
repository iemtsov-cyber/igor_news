Investment opportunity

Hospitality, events and corporate demand in one established asset.

P.T. Gardens Hotel is an established hospitality and event property in Walgama / Biyagama, approximately 1 km from the Biyagama Free Trade Zone, around 45 minutes from Colombo and approximately one hour from Bandaranaike International Airport.

Corporate base

Proximity to the Biyagama Free Trade Zone supports a natural corporate and long-stay customer base.

Events platform

Four banquet and function halls serve weddings, conferences, seminars and private events.

Operating asset

The hotel has operated since 1992 and combines accommodation, F&B, events and supporting infrastructure.

A substantial hospitality and events property positioned near one of Sri Lanka's major industrial zones.
Asset snapshot

Multiple revenue streams under one roof.

The investment case combines real-estate value with going-concern business value: hotel accommodation, weddings and events, corporate functions, food & beverage and long-term corporate / expatriate stays.
